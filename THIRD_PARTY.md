# Third-party components

Every dependency and asset, with licence and the check made before adopting it. Praxis Sound
itself is **MIT OR Apache-2.0** (owner, 2026-11-12; `LICENSE-MIT`, `LICENSE-APACHE`), so every
component must allow distribution under those terms: permissive licences only, enforced by
`cargo deny check` (`deny.toml`). Checked against crates.io on 2026-11-10; audited again for the
release on 2026-11-12 (WP-Z, `docs/release/licences.md`). The licence texts shipped with the app
are in `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt` (generated by `docs/release/notices.py`, bundled by the
installer, shown in the app under Licences).
